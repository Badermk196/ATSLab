## Summary of patterns

1. **Most complaints are about money, not software.** The recurring theme across Simplify, AIApply, Sprout, JobCopilot, Massive and GlobalWorkAI is subscription billing: surprise renewals, charges after cancelling, refunds refused. The second theme is auto-apply quality — irrelevant or wrong-location jobs, no interviews, and AI resume text that invents experience.
2. **Thin samples are everywhere.** Chrome Web Store scores rest on 2-26 ratings (Scale.jobs 4, Tsenta 3, Sprout 2); Sprout's Trustpilot has 3 reviews; Sorce, Tsenta, JobHunt, Scale.jobs and RolePrive all have Trustpilot profiles with zero reviews. Any score under about 10 reviews should not be quoted as a customer verdict.
3. **App stores inflate, Trustpilot deflates.** The same product can score 4.7 on the App Store and 1.5 on Trustpilot (Massive), 4.6/4.4 versus 2.8 (Sprout), 4.8 versus 2.5 (Simplify). Several profiles show near-uniform 5-star clusters (Find My Profession 94%, Scale.jobs 86%, JobXDubai 99% with zero criticism), and some companies publish figures higher than their own live page.

## Reliability caveats for the blog post

- **No public rating found at all:** Resumax (resumax.ai), ApplySent (applysent.com), RolePrive (roleprive.com).
- **GlobalWorkAI:** Trustpilot shows 203 reviews but suppresses the score after removing fake reviews — a red flag worth naming, though there is no score to quote.
- **Unverified or blocked pages:** Product Hunt for Massive and Resumax; Trustpilot for ApplySent and JobHunt (JobHunt's figures came from Trustpilot's own search page).
- **Conflicting numbers on the same page:** Simplify 2.5/2.7, Massive 1.5/1.7, JobCopilot 1.5/1.7 and 100/102 reviews, Naukrigulf 143K/145K, Sprout 140K/138K, JobHunt 6.91K/6.86K, Find My Profession 786/788.
- **Excluded deliberately:** two same-name G2 listings (Simplify 5.0 from 1 review; Massive 4.9 from 14 reviews) that describe different companies, and Scale.jobs' own claim of "4.7 on Trustpilot" which does not match the 4.5 shown.
- **Same-name products were disambiguated:** Simplify = simplify.jobs (not the other Simplify companies); Sprout = usesprout.com; Massive = usemassive.com (not the proxy-API vendor); Sorce = sorce.jobs; JobHunt = jobhunt.work; GlobalWorkAI = globalwork.ai (globalworkai.com is a parked domain for sale).
